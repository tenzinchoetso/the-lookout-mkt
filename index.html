Open since December 2025

A whitewashed rooftop café & bistro in the Tibetan Colony of Majnu-ka-Tilla, made for long lunches, sunset coffee and dinners under warm lights.

The kitchen moves easily from Tibetan momo and thenthuk to Bhutanese ema datshi, Indo-Chinese, North Indian curries, pasta and pizza, with coffee, shakes and coolers from the counter. You come up for the view and stay for the food.
