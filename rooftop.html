Our story

A café with a view, tucked into the Tibetan Colony.

The Lookout opened its doors in December 2025 on a rooftop in Block 11, New Camp, in the Tibetan Colony of Majnu-ka-Tilla. The building wears white, curving balconies and arches; inside there are wooden ceilings, woven lamps and soft sofas; outside, an open deck looks out over the Yamuna to the Signature Bridge.

“If peace had an address, it would be The Lookout Cafe.”

Come for a slow coffee in the afternoon, stay as the sky turns pink, and watch the lamps come on. The kitchen keeps it easy: momo and thukpa, chilli chicken and Hakka noodles, butter chicken, pasta and wood-board pizzas.
